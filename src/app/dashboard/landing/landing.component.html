Welcome to the Climate Action Navigator (CAN)

CAN is your entry point to exploring climate solutions at the local level. CAN offers high-resolution data and insights on key climate indicators, from heating emissions to walkability, helping you identify both strengths and areas for improvement in your city or region.

A few notes before you begin…

To reduce our carbon footprint, CAN employs lazy computing for more efficient data processing. This can sometimes impact performance, with computations taking up to 90 minutes during high traffic.

Our tools are adapted to urban areas, where free and open source data quality is better. Results in rural areas may be less accurate.

While CAN is open to everyone, our plugins are tailored to experts. As such plugins and assessment tools may include technical terms.

All tools and assessments within CAN are works in progress and will be continuously improved as new methods and datasets become available.
